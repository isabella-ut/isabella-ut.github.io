Working papers

Transit Captives: Gendered Risk of Sexual Harassment in Public Mobility Spaces — Bogotá as a Case Study
with Sofía García-Durrer

Examines how structural mobility constraints — trip complexity, public transport use, night travel, socioeconomic stratum — shape gendered harassment risk in Bogotá, using trip-level microdata from the Bogotá Mobility Survey (EMB 2023).

Working paper · presented at the 34th IAFFE Annual Conference, 2026

Projects

  • Building Local Resilience in Syria (BLRS) — ISDC. Impact evaluation of a UK-funded resilience and agricultural livelihoods programme for Syrian farming households.
  • Resilience and Crisis Response in Earthquake-Affected Areas in Syria and Türkiye — ISDC. Assessing crisis-response outcomes for populations affected by the 2023 earthquakes.

  • Women's Participation in Value Chains in PDET Municipalities — CoreWoman. Strengthened gender equity in firms across conflict-affected territories in Colombia.
  • Gender-Smart Investing Capacity Building Facility — CoreWoman. Designed a gender-lens investing strategy for the Yunus Social Business Fund.
