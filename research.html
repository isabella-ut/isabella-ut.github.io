Working papers

Transit Captives: Gendered Risk of Sexual Harassment in Public Mobility Spaces — Bogotá as a Case Study
with Sofía García-Durrer

Examines how structural mobility constraints — trip complexity, public transport use, night travel, socioeconomic stratum — shape gendered harassment risk in Bogotá, using trip-level microdata from the Bogotá Mobility Survey (EMB 2023).

Working paper · presented at the 34th IAFFE Annual Conference, 2026
