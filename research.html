Working papers

Transit Captives: Gendered risk of sexual harassment and assault in public mobility spaces — Bogotá as a case study
with Sofía García-Durrer

Examines how structural mobility constraints (such as trip complexity, public transport use, night travel, socioeconomic stratum) shape gendered harassment risk in Bogotá, using trip-level microdata from the Bogotá Mobility Survey (EMB 2023).

Working paper · presented at the 34th IAFFE Annual Conference, 2026

Projects

  • Impact evaluation of the UK-funded Building Local Resilience in Syria (BLRS) project at ISDC, which assessed the effects of different interventions in welfare and women's empowerment outcomes.
  • Impact evaluation of the project Resilience and Crisis Response in Earthquake-Affected Areas in Syria and Türkiye at ISDC, which measured the effects of health, education and livelihood interventions on welfare outcomes.
  • Impact evaluation of the project Restoring Food and Nutrition Security in Sudan at ISDC, which assessed the effects of agricultural interventions on welfare and food security outcomes.
  • Building the food-system platform PlaSa Colombia at Javeriana University for the Alliance of Bioversity International and the International Center for Tropical Agriculture, centralizing public and private data and creating interactive Tableau dashboards.
  • Project Finances with a Gender Lens at CoreWoman, which evaluated (using a synthetic control method) a financial-inclusion program for women.
  • Project Transforming the Care Economy through Impact Investing at CoreWoman, which documented care-economy enterprises across the Global South to mobilize capital to the sector.
  • Project Women's Participation in Value Chains in PDET Municipalities at CoreWoman, which strengthened gender equity in firms across conflict-affected territories in Colombia.
  • Project Gender-Smart Investing Capacity Building Facility at CoreWoman, which designed a gender-lens investing strategy for the Yunus Social Business Fund.
