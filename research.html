Research

Working papers

Transit Captives: Gendered Risk of Sexual Harassment in Public Mobility Spaces — Bogotá as a Case Study
with Sofía García-Durrer

Examines how structural mobility constraints — trip complexity, public transport use, night travel, socioeconomic stratum — shape gendered harassment risk in Bogotá, using trip-level microdata from the Bogotá Mobility Survey (EMB 2023).

Working paper · presented at the 34th IAFFE Annual Conference, 2026

Projects

International Security and Development Center (ISDC) 2024 – 2026
isdc.org

Impact evaluations of livelihood, gender, and agricultural interventions in Syria, Türkiye, and Sudan, including Building Local Resilience in Syria, Resilience and Crisis Response in Earthquake-Affected Areas in Syria and Türkiye, and Restoring Food and Nutrition Security in Sudan.

PlaSA Colombia 2022 – 2023
Pontificia Universidad Javeriana · alliancebioversityciat.org

CoreWoman 2021 – 2023
corewoman.org

Gender-lens finance and care-economy research in Colombia, including a synthetic-control evaluation of a financial-inclusion program for women and a gender-equity study of firms in conflict-affected territories.
